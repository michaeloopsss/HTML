Hokaido Island

Hokaido is the second largest island of Japan and the largest and northernmost prefecture. The Tsugaru Strait separates Hokkaido from Honshu. The two islands are connected by the undersea railway Seikan Tunnel. Hokkaido is Japan's 21st largest island, and the 22nd most populous island in the world and the 4th most populous island in Japan after Honshu, Kyushu and Shikoku.




Geography

Hokkaido is the northernmost of Japan's four main islands. It is located north of Honshu across the Tsugaru Strait and southwest of Sakhalin across the La Pérouse Strait. The island is roughly rectangular in shape, measuring approximately 360 km from east to west and 250 km from north to south, with a total area of 83,456.58 km² (32,210.77 sq mi), making it the second largest island of Japan and the largest and northernmost prefecture. The population density is low compared to other parts of Japan, with much of the island covered by forests, mountains, and wetlands.
